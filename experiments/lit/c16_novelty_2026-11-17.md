# C16 novelty check (2026-11-17, at C16 close)

Bence: "check whether our epx discoveries are already published". Method: 8 web searches (snippets and abstracts only, no full text) plus the C16 literature map (`c16_map_2026-11-17.md`). Train-only, exploratory findings; no C16 claim exists.

## Exposure note (data source)
- Three searches returned snippets of the **results** of Colgan et al. 2026 (Cell, doi 10.1016/j.cell.2026.09.050; bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.05.07.722278), the source paper of the embryo set. Standing rule: its results stay unread while B and C are sealed.
- What was seen, in aggregate across all 16 embryos (no per-embryo or per-partition numbers):
  - heritable expression grouped into about 70 lineage-associated programs;
  - anterior-posterior relationships set in the epiblast persist and are more heritable than dorsal-ventral ones;
  - clade timing correlates weakly with descendants' mean A-P position (R2 0.21);
  - earliest biases are mostly mesodermal;
  - the authors conclude that lineage, spatial position and signalling jointly set fate and its timing, with weights that vary by tissue.
- Nothing seen gives a P/L statistic for E9.0-R3 (B) or E10.0-R1 (C). No B or C look is planned (C16 closed). After this, further searches excluded cell.com. Any future use of B or C for a lineage-programme or position question must state this exposure.

## Bearing on earlier chapters
- **C14 (confirmed on A: state beyond cell type predicts clade fate in these embryos):** Colgan's "heritable lineage-associated programs" are close to this in substance. C14's held-out, pre-registered test on a sealed embryo is the distinct part; the biology overlaps. The C14 write-up should cite Colgan 2026 for this.
- **C7/C8, C10, C12:** not re-searched today; their cross-check stands in `novelty_map_2026-11-12.md` and DISCOVERIES.md. LaVOUS 2026 and scPFA 2026 are new method neighbours to add there.

## What is left that looks new
1. A held-out-embryo test design (one look per sealed embryo, power gated) for lineage claims, rather than within-data description.
2. The time control: the hidden state survives depth-varying rates, with a half-life of about 10 divisions.
3. The quantified separation of family bias from expression-inferred position per embryo.
These are methodological, not biological, contributions. The biology of C16 (inherited fate bias, heritable programmes, timing) is published, mostly on the same data.
