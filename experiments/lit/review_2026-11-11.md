## Bottom line

- The confirmed results are **solid but confirm what the field already reports.** Their value is the
  disciplined replication, not discovery.
- The open questions (carrier, coverage, memory dynamics, human instruction) need same-cell multiome clone
  data. That data is arriving in 2026 preprints (DeepTrack, hESC endoderm) but is not public yet.
