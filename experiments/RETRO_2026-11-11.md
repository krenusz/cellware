**What chapter 7 has shown (train, exploratory):**
- Clones carry a heritable fate state: sister wells agree beyond chance.
- That state is already readable before differentiation, in early RNA and in early chromatin, in both systems.
- **Chromatin is not the privileged carrier.** In blood, RNA carries more; in reprogramming, they are equal.
  The working hypothesis (chromatin first) is not supported on train in either system.

- **Verdict:**
  - The final pipeline was **chosen** without fate: validity 0.13 -> 0.78.
  - The **search** for fixes was triggered by a fate result. It only ran in one direction: making ATAC less
    blind, which could only raise G_ATAC.
  - The C7-S3 Delta is therefore **exploratory**. Its direction (RNA > chromatin) went against the fixes'
    direction, which makes it more credible, not less.
- **Should C7-S3 close?** Yes, as its pre-registered Delta can't be powered on the sealed partitions in either
  system. More train-side tuning would be amendment 5 and a forking path.

**What worked:**
- Nothing sealed was touched across 5 steps and 2 systems.
- Every amendment was logged before its run.
- The pipeline choice was made with a fate-free rule.
- The power gate stopped 2 unpowerable looks.
- The second system was run under the fixed pipeline with no fate-driven changes.
