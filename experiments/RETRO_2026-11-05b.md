# Retro, 2026-11-05 (late): full cycle E45-E50, B28-B35, and the goal reset

Covers the whole day, with focus on what happened after the earlier retro (RETRO_2026-11-05.md):
B33, the two papers, E49, B34, B35, E50 (running), and Bence's goal statement.

## Where we are against the goals

- **Long term** (any stem-cell fate, eventually embryo morphogenesis): no direct progress today;
  the reusable gains are methods. These are the scoring protocol, the held-out discipline, the
  every-condition check, the discoveries register, and a stopping rule that keeps one data set
  from absorbing the project.
- **Current step** (fit conditional data): partly met. The plausible-selection model (E48)
  reproduces the fate structure (B7 G, G-M agreement, cytokine N-shares) and is stable on the fit
  set. It does not reproduce three presence features: M-CSF small private clones, G-CSF
  neutrophil private clones, Epo/SCF sharing.
- **Short term** (instruct vs select): answered within the model family. Both act. Selection on a
  day-2 fate state carries most of the effect. Instruction is about 0.5 logits each way and has
  not moved across E38, E47, E48 or E49.

## What went well

- **Literature turned into constraints.** Rieger 2009 (and its supplement) bounded the selection
  strength. Lin/Naik 2024 gave a biological candidate for small restricted clones. Grover 2014
  gives one for the Epo wells (see cross-check).
- **Descriptive diagnostics found real structure fast.** B31 to B35 each took one run and each
  changed the picture: the co-presence deficit is a small-clone effect; small M clones are private
  and real; G-private clones are neutrophil-committed; day-2 abundance predicts seeding breadth.
- **Designs were rejected before fits.** The restricted M-class failed its every-condition check
  and was never run. The held-out test was not spent when B30 and B33 showed it could not answer
  the question.
- **Bence's challenges improved the record twice** (B29 audit, E47 amendment), and the goal
  question produced the stopping rule.

## What went badly

- **Model iteration ran ahead of the question.** E45, E49 and E50 chase presence features that
  have not moved the instruction estimate. Without Bence's question this would have continued.
  The stopping rule now prevents it.
- **Two flawed scoring rules reached the log** before being caught (B29, E47). Both were caught by
  Bence. Lesson already recorded (DISCOVERIES M1-M3).
- **A design assumption was never checked against the paper's design.** Discovery 7 (the full
  cocktail seeded from a separate clone pool, E37) has been carried since 2026-11-04. The SI and
  Supp Fig 7 both state that the day-2 cells were split evenly into four wells, Mix included. The
  "separate pool" is a fitted structure, not the design. See cross-check.
- **E49 was registered on one favourable starting point** (E45's best fit). The fit drifted away
  from it. Lesson: when a mechanism works only at one parameter point, probe whether the fit can
  stay there (cost) before registering.
- **Tooling:** the launcher exit code still reads as failure on success and cancel; the analysis
  scripts are copied per experiment (analyze_e47..e50) instead of being parametrised once.

## Decisions

1. **Stopping rule applies from E50.** E50 is judged on its pre-registered readings, then on
   whether it moves instruction (tau shifts) or gamma beyond their spread across E38/E47/E48.
   If not, the LARRY modelling stops here.
2. **Discovery 7 is reclassified as "in tension".** Any future write-up describes the full-arm
   structure as a fitted property, and names the growth-based alternative.
3. **Epo test queued (cheap data check, no model):** Epo presence and sharing with all cell
   types counted vs N/M only.
4. **After E50: write-up of the LARRY answer** (estimates with ranges across standing models,
   evidence, limits), then the held-out look (strong vs plausible/no selection), then choose the
   next conditional data set (a step toward "any stem-cell fate").
5. **Tooling:** one parametrised analysis script for future experiments; fix the launcher exit
   code.
