Chapter 5 is parked, data-limited (Bence: "close (park) cpt5"). It produced the memory model
(`core_model.memory_through`: ES clones keep their state through divisions and lose about half at each passage,

`WRITEUP_MEMORY.md`. Nothing sealed was read in chapter 5.

sealed. Cross-fitting by replicate made the passage-1 axis estimate honest.
