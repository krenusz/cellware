## 3. Scientific analysis

**Why chapter 6 failed, in one sentence:** we built the pole and the clone placement as two processes (a Wnt field,
then clones finding it), while the gastruloid literature says they are one process: cells with a posterior state
*are* the pole once they gather.

- **McNamara et al. 2024 (Nat Cell Biol; Wnt recording):** early Wnt-high and Wnt-low cells already differ; Wnt-high
  cells end up posterior by **sorting with differential adhesion**. Symmetry breaking is a sorting of
  pre-existing state heterogeneity, not a field that forms first.
- **Guan, ..., Hormoz 2025 (bioRxiv 2025.09.27.678924):** in an agent-based model, **short-range adhesion alone
  never gave a robust single axis** (asymmetry <= 0.83; robust region empty); adding **long-range attraction among
  one population** made ~18% of parameter space robust. Adhesion-only systems form several clusters: exactly our
  C6-S2 patches (homotypic 0.88, polar 0.06).
- **Ho et al. 2025 (arXiv 2509.08929):** elongation from local polarity alignment (convergent extension) in
  identical cells; elongation does not need fate differences. Fits our finding that elongation is the easy part.
- **Hashmi 2022 (eLife):** E-cadherin islands flow to the tip by collective movement; state and position co-evolve.

**Reading for our model:** C6-S2 (state-dependent adhesion only) is Guan's failing class; C6-S3 to S5 added a
separate Wnt field, which fixed the pole but decoupled it from state. The literature-backed design is **state ->
posterior identity -> short-range differential adhesion + long-range attraction among posterior cells**: placement
and pole become the same event, so the trade-off should disappear. This would be a redesign from published
mechanism, not a rule added for a misfit, but it would have to be pre-registered as such and still has **no
sealed test** (finding 1).

**Link between chapters 5 and 6:** chapter 5's passage loss (about half per passage) is what kills S1 in chapter 6:
by passage 4 clones 1 and 6 are indistinguishable under the core. Together with rho_g 0.059 (most of a gastruloid's
clone state is per-gastruloid noise, consistent with the heritable founder fluctuations of Regalado 2025),
**clone identity is a weak per-gastruloid predictor except for clones far apart in state (6 vs 7, 3 vs 6)**. Any
future spatial test should use such far-apart pairs at early passage.

**What would make chapter 6 testable:** a sealed or new set with (a) far-apart clone pairs at passage 1-2 and (b)
same-plate pure and mixed wells. None is known to be public. Ayyappan 2025 (the source lab) reports clone bias held
over several aggregation rounds; its spatial-transcriptomics data, if released, is the natural target.
