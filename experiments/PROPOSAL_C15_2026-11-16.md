## Why a new dataset is needed
- petracer_embryo has only B and C left: two E7.5 embryos, one E9.0 and one E10.0.
- The C14 effect is about 0 at E7.5-E8.0, so a B look is probably underpowered. H-c and H-d face the same limit.
- What would open the next door is a lineage + state dataset with many independent units. Many units means fresh sealed partitions and real power.
