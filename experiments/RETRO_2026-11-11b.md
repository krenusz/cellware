## Outcome

- **2 of 3 planned sealed tests confirmed** (T1 rho 0.45, T3 rho 0.59). T2 was refused by the power gate.
- **First chapter since chapter 3 to end with a confirmed primary result** that was chosen *for* being
  achievable. Chapter 7 also confirmed, but only after a rescue.
- **Effort:** about 4 steps (C8-S0 scan, C8-S1 selection, C8-S2 looks, one amendment).

## What worked

- **H19 gate.** Scanning power on train before choosing removed the "build first, find no power later"
  failure of chapters 4, 5 and 7.
- **The oracle power gate is the real safety net.** It recounts B structurally before spending, and it caught
  T2 without burning the partition.
- **Fixed sequence with a safe lead test.** T1 guaranteed a result. The riskier test came second.
- **Count functions read no fate column**, so refusing T2 cost no exposure.

## What went wrong

1. **Plan-stage power for a subset used the dataset-wide ratio.** T2 (exp 7 only) was estimated at 40
   effective B units with the flat 0.5 chspctrack ratio. The structural count was 32 (power 0.86 -> 0.78).
   K1 should not have passed the H19 scan.
2. **Label confusion (K vs T)** cost Bence time. Candidates and tests got two numbering schemes.
3. **The scan run first crashed on an assert** (two day-5 experiments). Harmless, but it was a missed
   pre-flight on experiment structure.

## Rule changes

- **H19 amended (program.md):** the plan-stage power for any test on a subset (an experiment, condition or
  stratum) must come from that subset's own structural count in the sealed partition. That count reads no fate
  labels and goes through the oracle count function or the same code. Dataset-wide ratios are no longer
  allowed for subsets.
- **Naming:** a chapter's tests are named only by their question label in user-facing text (e.g. "T1
  Neu/Mono"). Internal candidate IDs stay in the log.
