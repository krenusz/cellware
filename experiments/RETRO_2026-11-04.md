# Retro, 2026-11-04: E33-E39 and B17-B20

One page. Details in EXPERIMENT_LOG.md, findings in FINDINGS_2026-11.md.

## What went well

- **Measured inputs beat free latents.** Replacing the fitted clone trait with the measured day-2
  Cebpa - Irf8 score (E33) was the single biggest improvement: G-M went from out of reach to
  reproduced.
- **Literature priors helped identifiability.** Fixing instruction signs (E35) removed a
  wrong-sign solution that B17 had diagnosed, and gave the first full pass.
- **Diagnostics before models.** B18, B19 and B20 each answered a sharp question in minutes on
  this PC and redirected the next model. B20 pinned the B7 G shortfall to one clone group.
- **Cheaper compute.** Parallel generations and early stopping (cca1cc8) halved even short
  runs; the public repo lets 18 seeds run at once for free. 18-seed runs now take about an hour.
- **Pre-registration held.** Every verdict was written before the data were seen. The held-out
  shard is still unspent, by rule, after two power checks.

## What went badly

- **Warm starts trapped seeds** in the previous basin (E37 seeds 0-2). Cold starts found the
  separate-pool solution. Default to cold starts when the model gains a new structural parameter.
- **We kept adding mechanisms** (E36, E37, E39) while the core problem was a trade-off between
  G-M, B7 G and the within-condition levels. E39 showed a new mechanism is used as a substitute
  for an old one rather than adding information. The seed scatter is likely an identifiability
  ridge; we should have tested that before E39.
- **We waited for known verdicts.** E39 was decided after 11 of 18 seeds but ran to the end.
  Fixed: `run_sweep.sh --guard N` cancels a sweep once its stability rule cannot be met
  (standing rule from today).
- **Small-number statistics slipped in as targets.** Full-M depends on about 8 informative clones
  (B19); several models were judged partly on it. Next time, check how many clones drive a
  statistic before using it as a gate.
- **Infrastructure noise.** A network drop killed one watcher; GitHub returned push errors.
  Watchers now retry, and pushes are verified with `git ls-remote`.

## Decisions

1. **Stop adding mechanisms to this model family.** E38 is the reference model.
2. **Next analyses are about identifiability and simulator-free checks** (ridge profile or SBI,
   B21 fluctuation analysis, B22 MDP signature), not new simulators.
3. **Keep the held-out shard unspent** until a model's prediction for it is stable.
4. **Standing rules confirmed:** no outreach; pre-register; one look; adequacy gate before any
   cross reading; early-stop guard on every sweep; cold starts after structural changes.
