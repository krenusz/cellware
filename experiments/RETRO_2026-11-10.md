**What chapter 6 has learned for real (mechanism, synthetic):**
1. **One posterior pole needs three things together:**
   - adhesion energies that keep the body attached (partial wetting);
   - a long-range pull among posterior cells;
   - enough time (about 200k MCS).

   Adhesion alone always gives 2-3 caps. This matches the literature: patches must merge, and something
   long-range merges them.
2. **Elongation needs force across the whole body.** Posterior-only crawling never elongates, whatever its
   polarity (C6-S7f, k, l-b).
3. **The order matters for the data checks.** Elongation that starts before the pole picks a random axis. The
   A-P line scan then runs along the wrong axis, and the boundary tracks composition (Q2 failure).
4. **The clone-state and identity rule is not the problem** (C6-S7i D1). In perfect geometry it gives fixed
   domains (slope 0.07), as the data do.

## 3. Literature gap map (`lit/gap_2026-11-10.md`)

- **Seeds extended** with the papers C6-S7 adopted: Guan/Hormoz 2025, de Jong 2024, Suppinger 2023, Serrano
  Najera 2025, Trani-Bustos 2025. The spatial-mechanism topic gains convergent extension, intercalation and PCP.
- **Scale:** 7,108 works in the neighbourhood of 41 core papers; 2,624 relevant to two or more open questions.
- **Top 40 unread:** 39 open access, 1 paywalled. That one is a Company of Biologists "In preprints" piece on
  lineage tracing and is not needed. **No subscription is justified.**
- **Read now (abstracts via OpenAlex), what matters for chapter 6:**
  - **Marangoni-like tissue flows enhance symmetry breaking of embryonic organoids** (Nat Phys 2025, open
    preprint), and **Marangoni flows underlie symmetry breaking in early mouse gastruloids** (Nat Phys 2025,
    *paywalled, Springer Nature*).
    - These name a physical mechanism for our weakest ingredient. A **surface-tension gradient drives a
      tissue-scale flow** that gathers the posterior cells: a biological candidate for Guan's
      phenomenological 1/d^2 pull (and Hashmi's tissue flow).
    - **This is the most important unread item.** Read the open preprint before any next model step. The
      paywalled version is the only paper worth access; it can be requested via interlibrary or institutional
      access, not by contacting anyone.
  - **Cadherins modulate the self-organizing potential of pseudo-embryos** (Cell Rep 2025, open).
    - A coordinated E- to N-cadherin switch (Snai1) is needed for self-organisation. This supports the adhesion
      ingredient (partial wetting between E-cad and N-cad cells).
    - N-cadherin loss lets gastruloids form somites without matrix. Adhesion also tunes elongation.
  - **Fine-tuning mechanical constraints...** (Development 2025, open).
    - Ultra-soft embedding keeps elongation and patterning; stiffer embedding blocks polarisation while
      expression is unchanged. **Impaired cell motility underlies the polarisation defect.**
    - It agrees with our finding that motility and pull, not identity, build the pole.
  - **Early autonomous patterning of the A-P axis in gastruloids** (Development 2024, open). T polarisation can
    start before the Wnt pulse and is robust to size. Our model assigns identity at t 0; this says the early
    state matters (relevant to the outer start).
- **Also unread and relevant:** PTK7 / PCP and convergent extension in mouse gastrulation (2009, 2007). Background
  for arm (c), the mediolateral crawling.
