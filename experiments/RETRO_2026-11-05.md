# Retro, 2026-11-05: E45-E48, B28-B32, literature on growth and death

## What went well

- **Literature turned into a usable bound.** Rieger 2009 and its supplement (GMPs: ~19% early
  death in both M-CSF and G-CSF; ~2 doublings on day 1, slower on day 2; 75% commit by the first
  division) gave a plausible range for selection strength, gamma ~0.3-0.6. That converted E46's
  "good fit" into a testable claim, and E47/E48 tested it.
- **Bounded variants answered the question cleanly.** E48 (gamma <= 0.6) ties E46 on B7 G with
  plausible biology; E47 (<= 0.35) shows the strict end is too tight. In both, the fit pushes
  gamma to the cap.
- **Data diagnostics beat model iteration again.** B31 located the co-presence deficit in 2-cell
  clones in one run; B32 showed those clones are private to the M-CSF well, real (spread over
  libraries) and the source of the long-standing M-presence misfit. Four model variants (E43-E46)
  had been chasing this.
- **The held-out test was protected.** B30 showed the Epo/SCF look could not separate models
  (difference 0.07 vs spread 0.7); it was skipped, not spent.
- **Speed:** fit 2.1x faster (cef1edb) with byte-identical statistics.

## What went badly

- **Two flawed scoring rules, both caught by Bence, not by me.** (1) The primary concordances
  used held-out clones and pass/fail rewarded wide predictions (B29 audit). (2) The E47 decision
  summed CRPS over statistics with no information, so noise picked the reference (amendment).
  Lesson: **before registering a comparison, check each statistic's simulated width against its
  scale; leave uninformative statistics out of any sum.**
- **E46 was called "the best model" too early,** and B31 then showed it fitted co-presence for the
  wrong reason (compensating errors in small and large clones). Lesson: a fit to an aggregate
  statistic needs a decomposition check before it is credited.
- **Launcher fragility.** Starting run_sweep.sh with `&` killed it before the download (E47);
  results had to be fetched by hand. Run it as a background task, as done for E48.
- **Two shell-quoting failures** (nested heredoc + python -c) cost a retry; long pre-registrations
  now go through a scratch file.

## Decisions

1. **Model status:** E46 is the best fit to the current targets, but its strong selection is
   implausible and its co-presence fit is compensatory. **E48 is the working model** (plausible
   selection, B7 G fitted). E38 is the no-selection-coupling baseline.
2. **Instruction answer so far:** across E38, E47 and E48, instruction stays at ~0.5 logits each
   way while selection varies, so in this data both act, together. No claim of a ratio yet.
3. **Next model (E49):** an explicit M-CSF-private small-clone class (or presence targets at
   >= 3 cells), fixing B7 G with E48's plausible selection. Also address the 2x over-sharing with
   Epo/SCF. Pre-register with every-condition checks and minimum counts.
4. **Held-out:** the Epo/SCF shard 1 stays sealed. A B7-type statistic on shard-1 clones gets its
   own power check first.
5. **Scoring protocol:** fit-set primary; CRPS comparisons only over informative statistics
   (currently B7 G + B7 M); decompose any aggregate fit before crediting it.
