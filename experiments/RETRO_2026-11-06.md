### The three alignment problems

1. **No shared model.** The chapter-2 question was whether *the same* model generalises. A shared
   core is needed: clone fate state z, per-condition (or per-arm) instruction shift, growth
   selection on z, seeding/presence, detection. LARRY E48 and SIS R2 should become two
   configurations of one code base, each refit through it. That is the real generalisation test,
   and the first concrete step toward "any stem-cell fate".
2. **The SIS-skew model leaves out a fact from the paper: Bcor editing efficiency was 40%**
   (Tian 2021, results and STAR Methods; "major clonal expansion in a proportion of Bcor-/Y
   clones"). The KO arm is a mixture of edited and unedited sisters. E51 treats it as fully
   edited, so gamma and tau are diluted (to about 0.4x if editing is per cell), and the "uniform
   10x expansion" is likely a mean over edited and unedited lines. Under the stopping rule this
   *can* move the answer by more than its current spread: it must be fixed before the chapter-2
   answer is written.
3. **Space is missing for the long-term goal.** Morphogenesis needs positions and interactions.
   No current data set or model has them. NEXT_DATASETS should add a spatial candidate
   (spatially resolved gastruloid or embryo lineage data), and the old tissue simulator should be
   reconnected as the spatial layer, fed by the clone-level fate models.

## 3. Decisions

1. **Before the chapter-2 write-up:** add editing efficiency to the SIS-skew model (edit
   probability per Cherry+ founder line, prior centred on 0.4), re-run R2 recovery and E51 on the
   fit set. The held-out shard for these statistics is spent, so the new fit is judged on the fit

   simulator takes the clone-level fate model as its cell policy prior.

**Net literature picture.** The clonal literature (Naik 2013, Lin 2018, Lin 2021, Weinreb 2020,
Calabria 2024) supports our central claim: a strong, heritable clone fate state on which
perturbations act mainly by selection. Single-cell imaging studies (Rieger 2009,
Mossadegh-Keller 2013) and in vivo overexpression (Tsapogas 2014) show instruction exists.
Our models quantify both and find instruction modest in LARRY. The one direct conflict to resolve
is Tian 2021's "gain of pDC fate potential" vs our tau about 0 (decision 2, B42).
