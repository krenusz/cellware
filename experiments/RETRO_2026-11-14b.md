## Second check (2026-11-14, Bence: "check the retro with opus") — missed in the first draft
5. **The S1 power numbers break the power rule.** H17 and program.md require selection power from the model's own simulated statistic at the sealed n, using only `mean_power`, `rho_power` or `sim_power`. `c12s1_select.py` used `power.z_power` with a bootstrap SE computed on fixed out-of-fold predictions, so the SE leaves out refit variance. The 0.55-0.57 per partition and **0.85 pooled A+B are provisional and probably optimistic.** Before any look is considered, power must be recomputed by simulation (refit the frozen pipeline on resampled train clones at the sealed n, `sim_power`).
6. **The label-shuffle bias was not subtracted.** The D40 label-shuffle delta was +0.016 (10 shuffles), so its +0.06 is closer to +0.04 net. The in vivo shuffle was -0.010, so that readout is unaffected.
7. **The retro rule was skipped.** Every retro runs `lit_gap.py`; this one reused the morning's map. Stated here as a deviation, not as compliance.
8. **The dataset id `lsc_larry` is itself an abbreviation of the sensitive term.** It is the partition key in `partitions.py` (the salt and seal depend on the entry name), so renaming it would touch the seal. Left as is; new text uses "C12".
