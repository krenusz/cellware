**Net:** the literature now supports chapter 3's strong result (heritable clone state), offers a
concrete mechanism for the unpredictable memory (methylation-sensitive, culture-dependent state), and
offers one testable mechanism for mixing rescue (Wnt-responsiveness, binary). The single most
important unread paper is the Cell 2026 study, and its data set is the one we already partitioned.
