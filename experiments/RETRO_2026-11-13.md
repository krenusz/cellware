## The core mistake
**The treatment is assigned per library, and there are only two libraries per arm.** Any drug-vs-control quantity
(instruction, drug-specific selection) is replicated at the library level, n = 2 per arm, or n = 1 once time
matching is imposed.
- Hundreds of lineages inside a library add precision, not replication of the treatment.
- This was visible in the 2026-11-12 map ("two replicates per arm") before any code.
- **H19 should have stopped C10 at S0.** "Primary test power >= 0.8 from the subset's own sealed counts" was
  read at the family level, but the contrast does not vary at the family level.

**Why it slipped:**
- The step was framed as a "scan, NOT a passed chapter gate", so the gate was never actually applied.
- Each amendment then narrowed the test (fewer arms, one pair) without re-asking H19.
- The placebo found by measurement what the design already implied.

## Secondary mistakes
- **Placebo P1 design error:** k = 30 components were chosen by sister AUC with no degrees-of-freedom cap; 31
  regressors against 36-75 families made F2 degenerate.
- **P2 was not matched for group count:** the control libraries have the fewest groups (39, 68), and small-n bias
  may inflate their logF.
- **Four amendments in one day on one step:** the H18 trigger was reached by design churn, not by learning from data.

## Lessons -> landing
1. **Rule H22 — ADOPTED 2026-11-14** (Bence: "go 3 adopt h22"; program.md, RULES_DIGEST.md):
   - Before any C*-S0 goes past structure, name the **treatment-assignment unit** of the primary contrast (the
     level at which the condition varies: library, well, animal, plate).
   - If it has **fewer than 3 independent units per arm**, and the question cannot be answered by a contrast
     inside one unit, **stop**. Lineage or cell counts cannot substitute.
   - Every amendment that drops arms or pairs re-checks this.
2. **Practice line:** any regression on learned features caps k at <= n_units / 5. Featuriser choice is limited
   to k values that meet the cap.
3. **Practice line:** library-comparison placebos are matched for unit count by subsampling to the smallest
   library.
4. **Memory:** C10 is the third chapter to end data-limited for a design reason visible at S0 (chapters 4 and 5
   were the others). Bence's anger is about exactly this.
